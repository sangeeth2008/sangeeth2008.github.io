1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy outlines how Sangeeth Sasikumar K S ("we", "us", or "operator") collects, handles, stores, and protects personal information when you visit and interact with the official engineering portfolio website located at https://sangeeth2008-github-io.vercel.app/.

We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining transparent data practices in compliance with global standards, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

2. Information Collected

We collect information in two limited ways:

  • Automatically Collected Technical Data: When you navigate the website, certain non-identifying technical information is processed, such as browser type, operating system, device screen resolution, approximate geographic region (country/city level, without precise GPS coordinates), referring URL, visit timestamp, and page interaction duration.
  • Voluntarily Disclosed Direct Communication: If you choose to contact us via direct email (sangeethcherur@gmail.com), GitHub, or LinkedIn, we receive the personal information you explicitly provide (such as your name, email address, and message contents).

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as financial account details, government identification numbers, biometrics, or protected demographic categories.

3. Purpose of Processing

The collected information is utilized strictly for the following purposes:

  • Monitoring website availability, performance, and server stability.
  • Analyzing aggregated traffic patterns to optimize the 3D WebGL experience across different devices and screen dimensions.
  • Responding to project collaborations, hiring inquiries, engineering feedback, and research discussions sent by visitors.
  • Detecting and mitigating malicious traffic, Denial of Service (DoS) attempts, and abusive automated scrapers.

4. Analytics & Infrastructure Services

To deliver and analyze this site, we utilize trusted third-party providers:

  • Google Analytics (gtag.js): Measures anonymous traffic and interaction events. IP anonymization is applied where mandated. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Google Fonts & CDN Services: Google Fonts (Space Grotesk, Outfit, JetBrains Mono) and CDNJS (Three.js WebGL library) are loaded to render modern technical typography and 3D graphics.
  • Hosting (Vercel / GitHub Pages): Edge infrastructure providers that serve static HTML/CSS/JS assets and maintain standard access server logs for operational security.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Retention Period: Aggregated analytical metrics collected via Google Analytics are automatically retained according to Google's standard retention cycle (up to 14 months) after which they are systematically purged. Direct communication emails are retained only as long as necessary to conduct project or professional correspondence.

Your Deletion & Access Rights: Regardless of your physical location, you have the right to:

  • Request confirmation whether any personal data concerning you is stored.
  • Request a copy of any correspondence records containing your information.
  • Request the immediate erasure ("right to be forgotten") of your correspondence and contact history from our local records.

To execute any deletion or data request, simply dispatch an email with your request to the legal contact address below. We process requests within 14 business days with zero fee.
