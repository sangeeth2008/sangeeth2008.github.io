1. What are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember user preferences, and provide aggregated analytics to site owners.

In addition to cookies, web browsers support Local Storage, which allows websites to store small client-side data pairs (such as dark mode preferences or audio toggles) directly on your device without transmitting data back to a server on every request.

3. How to Manage & Disable Cookies

You have the full right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies by default, but you can modify your browser settings to decline them or delete existing ones.

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies → Block third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection → Strict / Custom.
  • Apple Safari: Settings → Safari → Advanced → Block All Cookies.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies and site data.
  • Google Analytics Opt-out: You can download the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on to disable tracking on all sites.

Note: Disabling analytics cookies will not impair your ability to browse this portfolio or interact with the 3D robotics laboratory.
