(01)AboutWho builds this stuff

I build deterministic machines that sense, compute and actuate in real time: hardware that solves practical problems without waiting on the cloud.

I'm a B.Tech Mechatronics Engineering student at Jyothi Engineering College, Kerala, building real-world systems where electronics, robotics, IoT, embedded firmware and AI meet.

My approach combines mechanical kinematics with low-latency C/C++ firmware, distributed IoT telemetry and on-device TinyML inference. From differential-drive security rovers with multi-zone obstacle clearance to quantized vision models on microcontrollers, I care about machines that work reliably and on their own.

(04)JourneySix milestones

From first circuit to autonomous machines.

  1. 01

    Foundations

    B.Tech Mechatronics Engineering

    Building rigorous fundamentals in mechanical kinematics, circuit theory, CAD and actuator systems at Jyothi Engineering College, bridging hardware design with mathematical modelling.

  2. 02

    Embedded control

    C/C++ firmware & microcontrollers

    Deterministic, non-blocking state-machine firmware on ESP32 and Arduino, with hardware timer interrupts, dual-core FreeRTOS tasks and I²C, SPI and UART buses.

  3. 03

    Connectivity

    Internet of Things systems

    Bidirectional telemetry with MQTT brokers, Blynk dashboards and wireless MJPEG camera streaming for real-time remote sensing and actuation.

  4. 04

    Machine perception

    Edge AI & TinyML deployment

    Training and quantizing lightweight CNNs with Edge Impulse and TensorFlow Lite Micro, then running real-time classification on low-power microcontrollers.

  5. 05

    System resilience

    Hardware failsafes & security

    Watchdogs, brownout recovery, tilt-protection cutoffs and authenticated telemetry to protect cyber-physical systems from runtime anomalies.

  6. 06

    Next

    Autonomous intelligent robotics

    The ongoing mission: fully autonomous, self-navigating mobile robots and intelligent mechatronic machines that extend what people can do.
